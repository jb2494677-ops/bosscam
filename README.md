# 👀 BossCam

A tiny Mac menu bar app. When a second face shows up on your camera, it instantly brings your chosen app to the front.

## [⬇ Download BossCam for Mac](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/34163062/BossCam.zip)

Free · Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer)

## Install

1. Open the downloaded **BossCam.zip** (double-click it) and drag **BossCam** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Open BossCam. macOS will say it can't verify the app, because it isn't from the App Store. Click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about BossCam, click **Open Anyway** and enter your password.
4. Click **Allow** when it asks for camera access. A 👀 appears in your menu bar.

You only need to do steps 2 and 3 once.

## Using it

- Click the 👀 and choose **Choose app to switch to…** to pick the app it jumps to.
- The icon turns 🚨 when it switches. Tick "go back to my previous app" if you want it to return when you're alone.
- Use **Pause** when you're not gaming. That turns the camera fully off and saves battery.

## Good to know

- Everything runs on your Mac. No internet needed, nothing saved, nothing sent anywhere.
- It only counts faces. It doesn't recognise who anyone is.
- Works best when faces are lit from the front and looking toward the camera.
- macOS fades between full-screen apps on different desktops. That's a system animation BossCam can't remove.
